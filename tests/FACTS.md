## Time
- All passport services: 45 minutes. All family book services: 45 minutes. All smart ID card services: 20 minutes.

## Places
- Passport (all 4) and family book (all 4): Civil Status & Passports Department offices (مكاتب دائرة الأحوال المدنية والجوازات) AND Jordanian embassies/consulates abroad (السفارات والقنصليات).
- Smart ID card (all 4): ONLY the department offices — NOT embassies.

## Fees
- Passport first time: 20 JD military/security/civil defence/peacekeepers; 25 JD under 16; 50 JD inside Jordan; 100 JD outside Jordan.
- Passport renewal: 20 JD military etc. or marital-status change; 25 JD under 16; 20 JD full pages & still valid; 50 JD over 16 and expired or <180 days left; 100 JD not expired / >180 days left; 100 JD outside Jordan. Note: +25 JD at the urgent/express service office. Jerusalem ID holders pay like Jordanians.
- Passport lost: 125 JD first time; 250 JD any later time (+ previous guarantee collected if previous loss < 5 years ago). Documents include a financial guarantee (كفالة مالية) of 50–500 JD, police loss report, security approval, investigation committee approval, 2 colour photos.
- Passport damaged: 100 JD. Note: if damage was intentional → referred to the investigation committee (لجنة التحقيق).
- Family book first time: 2 JD; fine 10 JD if 90 days passed since the marriage contract.
- Family book renewal: 2 JD (دينارين) if expired; 3 JD if not expired. Note: must renew within 3 months of expiry; report data changes within 30 days.
- Family book lost: 5 JD; written pledge (تعهد) 10 JD; 3rd loss → judicial guarantee 50 JD. Only head of family (رب الأسرة) or wife may apply.
- Family book damaged: 3 JD. Condition: must not be intentionally damaged. Docs include written pledge 10 JD.
- Smart ID first time: 2 JD; 10 JD late fine if 18+ and never issued. Docs: 1 colour photo, blood-type test or written declaration, guardian approval if under 16.
- Smart ID renewal: 2 JD. Docs: form, the card being renewed. Applicant in person.
- Smart ID lost: 5 JD. Notes: card issued only 2 weeks after police report; 2nd loss within 3 years → pay 10 JD pledge; 3rd loss → investigation committee + 50 JD guarantee. Minors: guardian applies, no proxy/power of attorney (لا يقبل التوكيل). 1 colour photo.
- Smart ID damaged: 3 JD.

## Photos
- Passport (first time, renewal, lost, damaged): 2 colour photos. Smart ID first time / lost: 1 photo.

## Conditions worth testing
- A wife may get/renew a passport WITHOUT her husband's approval (دون موافقة زوجها).
- Passport: personal attendance for 18+; parents (father/mother) can apply for minor children.
- Smart ID: applicant in person.
